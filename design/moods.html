Meet Kettle, your workout buddy

Kettle lives in the widget on your home screen. Kettle starts the day friendly, then gets ruder (and rounder) the longer today's workout waits, and the picture beside Kettle makes the point without words. Work out and Kettle slims down and shows off. Drag the slider to see how Kettle looks through the day, then tap "Workout done".
